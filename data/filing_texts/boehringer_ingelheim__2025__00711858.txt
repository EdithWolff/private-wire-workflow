Strategic Report
The directors submit their Strategic Report on the affairs of the company for the year ended 31 December 2024.
Review of the Business

The company operates in the pharmaceutical industry which has a complex and regulated environment. Management use
and review many Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), including those mentioned below.

Turnover of £498.6m in 2024, relating entirely to the company's human pharmaceutical business, has risen significantly
from £346.2m due to significant volume growth in diabetes and respiratory products, a lower rebate percentage for the
new Voluntary Scheme for Branded Medicines Pricing, Access and Growth (VPAG) compared to its predecessor and,
accrued income in relation to overpaid output Value Added Tax on historic rebates paid by the company.

In 2024 the company made a profit before taxation of £28.6m, an increase from £12.2m in 2023 which is predominantly
driven by the volume growth above offset with an increase in distribution expenses.

Net assets increased from £45.3m to £56.9m as a result of higher stock holding in 2024 driven by customer demand and

sales growth. :

market:

Principal Risks and Uncertainties

The principal risks facing the business are increases in levels of the VPAG rebates, increased generic competition and
parallel trade, together with ongoing market access issues.

The new VPAG commenced on 1 January 2024 (replacing the Voluntary Scheme for Branded Medicines Pricing and
Access (VPAS)), as part of the scheme the company is required to pay a rebate based on a percentage of sales to the
Department of Health and Social Care. The rebate paid for 2024 was 15.13% for newer medicines and 10.03% for older
medicines (2023: VPAS 26.5% all medicines). An additional top up payment on older medicines, based on any observed
price decline, is due in 2025.

The risks regarding increased generic competition as well as ongoing market access issues are managed and mitigated by
continued research and development of new products and ongoing internal initiatives to enable the company to best
‘suppoft its customers, The company does not seek to encourage or prohibit parallel trade.

Interaction with stakeholders to promote the success of the Company

Under s172 (1) CA 2006, the directors have a duty to act in good faith and to promote the success of the company whilst
having regard to interactions with key stakeholders. As a pharmaceutical company, the needs and welfare of all patients is
at the centre of every decision taken. If the company is doing the right thing for the patient then this will only help the
success of the company going forwards.

The company also sees that its number one priority is its employees and takes into account employee needs and input in
major decisions by making use of its Information and Consultation Forum - please see the additional comments on
Employee Involvement in the Directors’ Report.

‘The company understands the need to have positive working relationships with all suppliers and regularly reports on its
payment practices iri the gov.uk portal. The company also has a supplier code of conduct which can be found on the
company's internet page.

Financial Risk Management

‘The company’s operations expose it to a variety of financial risks that include the effects of changes in price risk,
exchange risk, credit risk, liquidity risk, interest rate risk and cash flow risk. The company has in place a ris!
management programme that seeks to limit the adverse effects of movement in foreign currencies and cash flow risk by
monitoring levels of currency exposure and the related finance costs. The company does not use derivative financial
instruments to manage interest costs and as such, no hedge accounting is applied. The company had no bank loans or
overdrafts at the balance sheet date (2023: nil). The company monitors its credit risk by continually reviewing the
financial strength of both its suppliers and customers. The company has continued access to the parent company's cash-
pooling facility ensuring sufficient funds are available for operations.
