Economic risk

The Company deems there to be zero risk associated with the Ukraine and Russia conflict due to the absence
of any trade and/or connections to and from both countries.

Rising inflation, higher interest rates and general. uncertainty surrounding the UK economy could have an
impact on the Company's results and financial position. The Directors continue to monitor these factors and
consider what (if any) mitigations are required.

At the beginning of April 2025 the US introduced a global baseline tariff of 10% on nearly all imports, branded
the Global Reciprocal Tariff Regime, marking the most significant overhaul of its trade policy in decades. In
response certain other trading partners (China, Mexico, Canada and the EU) have introduced retaliatory
tariffs, whilst many others have not yet responded as they look to negotiate individual deals. The retaliatory
tariffs introduced largely do not apply to Hologic’s products and are mainly focussed on items such as US
steel, aluminium, auto and consumer goods like whisky, coffee, peanut butter and wine. As such the existing
exposure for Hologic based on known tariff impacts relates to products and materials currently imported into
the US.

At this point these tariffs will not be included in the standard cost for relevant US manufactured items and as
such they will not be passed through to any related party companies outside of the US, so-all costs will be
borne in the US. Therefore there will be no impact for the UK.

Climate change risk

The Company has considered the impact of climate change and has determined the potential risks for Hologic
Ltd as follows:

* Increasing frequency of extreme weather events (e.g. floods, droughts, heatwaves, strong winds)
causing disruption to manufacturing and other operations.

*  Anincreased number of very hot days (>35°C) resulting in reduced productivity. Extreme heat could
result in heat stress affecting our staff.

+ Uncertainty over future regulatory policy responses to address climate change that countries around
the world may develop, including carbon pricing.

Due to the nature of Hologic Ltd.'s business the Directors do not believe the risks listed above currently have
a material impact on the Company. Hologic aims to make business decisions that have a positive impact on
the environment and the.Company manages the environmental health and safety concerns as an integral part
of the business.

Quantification and Reporting Methodology

This report was compiled ‘internally by the Company's UK facility engineer. The 2019 UK Government
Environmental Reporting Guidelines and the GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard
were followed to ensure the Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (“SECR") requirements were met and
exceeded where possible.

The energy data was collated from a combination of invoices and mileage records. Where data was no
available the consumption was estimated based on a simple pro rata extrapolation.

The energy data was converted to carbon emissions using the 2024 UK..Government GHG Conversion
Factors for Company Reporting. The associated emissions are divided into mandatory and voluntary
emissions according to the 2018 Regulations, then further divided into the direct combustion of fuels and the
operation of facilities (scope 1), indirect emissions from purchased electricity (scope 2) and further indirect
emissions that occur as a consequence of Company activities (scope 3).

Intensity Measurement

The chosen primary intensity ratio is total gross emissions in metric tonnes CO2e (mandatory emissions) per
employee, which is the recommended ratio for the sector.

Events after the end of the reporting period

On 14 September 2025 a proposal to close Hologic Ltd.'s manufacturing facility was: approved. As a
result of the closure manufacturing will cease at the Manchester site by 31 December 20285. This will
result in 139 roles being made redundant along with the closure of the manufacturing building.

The Company is still assessing the total financial impact of the closure, however based on currently
available information the total financial impact is estimated to be in the range of £22m-£23m. Significant
costs within this estimate relate to severance packages, accelerated depreciation, obsolete inventory
write offs and onerous lease costs. It should also be noted that Hologic Ltd. operates as a contract
manufacturer on behalf of other group entities and therefore the majority of the costs associated with
the closure should be re-imbursed by other group entities. On this basis the final net financial impact on
Hologic Ltd. as a result of the closure should not be material.
